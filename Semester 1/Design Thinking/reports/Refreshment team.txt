Set up stalls/tables at the starting line, midway points, and finish line of the marathon.

Water, electrolyte drinks, glucose, energy bars, bananas, and light snacks that are easy to digest.

Hand out water bottles or cups to runners.

Keep energy drinks for those who need extra stamina.

Provide fruits like bananas, oranges, or energy bars to replenish energy.

Look for participants struggling or slowing down—guide them to the medical team if necessary.

Cleanliness: Place dustbins at every refreshment point to avoid littering. Volunteers should 
also keep the area neat.Collect used bottles/cups and keep area clean.

Distribution team: Hand out water and snacks quickly to runners.

Stock monitoring team: Ensure no shortage

Communicate with organizers and medical staff if any runner seems unwell.

Provide refreshments at the finish line for recovery (fruits, juice, light snacks).

Assist in post-event cleanup.

Support in distribution of refreshments to volunteers and attendees attending the awareness program.
