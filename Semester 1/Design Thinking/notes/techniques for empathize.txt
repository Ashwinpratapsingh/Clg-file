techniques for empathize step-
observation & shadowing- watch users in situations and observe and find hidden pain points/struggles/challenges
interviews- hear experiences to know needs
surveys- collected desired questions' answers at large scale
5 why + root cause analysis- find underlying issues
affinity diagram- cluster ideas/challenges into themes
customer journey mapping- map user's step by step interaction and spot friction points
case studies- learn from examples

techniques for define step-
review data collected from empathize step
map user journey
frame problem- problem that is human centered, actionable, focused
define pov which makes work easier

techniques for ideate step-
how might we question- reframing technique used in the question framed in define step
divergent thinking- generate multiple ideas by
		brainstorming - quick ideas in group
		brainwriting- silent writing ideas, then share
		SCAMPER- substitute, combine, adapt, modify, put to another use, eliminate, reverse
		worst possible idea- explore bad ideas to spark thinking
convergent thinking- after generating ideas, evaluate them by
		dot voting- vote for most promising task
		impact and feasibility matrix-
		prioritization grid- decide which idea to test first
selection of idea-
high potential idea
combine if necessary

techniques for prototype step-
from the most potential ideas in ideation, build low fidelity prototype
build sketch, paper model, cardboard model
keep model user focused
make blueprints for easy servicing

techniques for test step-
prepare for testing- select target users for testing
decide type of feedback needed, like, functionality, usability, or overall experience
let users interact with prototype
observe how they use it without much instructions, which helps in further refinement of the product
collect feedback by surveys, ratings, feedback forms
analyze results for refinements and repeat the model
