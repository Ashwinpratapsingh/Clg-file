My topic today is the journey of Nick Vujicic, a man born without arms and legs, yet living a life full of joy and happiness, and impact. He loves swimming, surfing, fishing, golfing. His story proves that our greatest limitations are not physical, but mental, he proves why he is specially abled in true sense. Nick once said, “If you can’t get a miracle, become one.” Those words teach us that self-belief is the foundation of strength. We all face moments when life feels unfair or impossible. But Nick shows us that challenges are not stop signs, they are the stepping stones. Nick went into depression, he had no hope, but then he states that he found his hope and strength in Jesus Christ, and we can find ours in anything. He completed his schooling and graduation, and reached where he is today. When we stop focusing on what we lack and start appreciating what we have, we unlock the power to rise again, stronger than before. If we fall 100 times and then try to stand back up 100 times, but then we give up, then our chances to rise up again are clearly zero, but if we will try again, there will always be a chance for us to rise up. So, the next time you feel like giving up, remember it’s not about how many times you fall, but how many times you choose to stand back up. Because what matters is how you finish, did you finish strong or did you finish weak. There are always 2 choices, a better choice and a bitter choice, If Nick can live without limbs yet stand tall with confidence, then each of us can overcome any obstacle with faith, effort, and a positive mindset, so we should never give up and never stop trying to rise again
