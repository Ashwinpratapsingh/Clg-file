aim- demonstration of breadboard,cro,dso,dmm and to study and practice soldering desoldering
apparatus- breadboard,cro,dso,dmm,soldering gun,soldering flux,solder wire
theory(cro dso)- breadboard- reusable,solderless device used to build and test electronic circuits by inserting components into a grid of holes, making temporary connection, no permament joints
cathode ray oscilloscope- provides accurate time and amplitude measurements of voltage signals over a wide range of frequencies. the heart of cro is cathode ray tube
digital storage oscilloscope- works by taking incoming analog signal, conditioning it with amplifiers and attenuators then using analog to digital converter(adc) to convert it into digital data, this digital data is stored in memory, processed by digital signal processor(dsp) then used to reconstruct waveform for display on screen
dmm- used to mesure ac volt, dc volt,ac current,dc current,resistance,capacitance,continuity of signals
theory(solder)- soldering iron uses an electrically heated tip to melt a low-melting-point metal alloy. The molten alloy flows between two heated workpieces. As it cools, the alloy solidifies, forming a strong, electrically conductive, and permanent bond between the components

apparatus- breadboard,dmm,variable dc motor supply(0-1v,0-10v),resistance(1kohm),diode(in4007),connecting wires
theory- pn junction diode- device made by joining p n type semiconductors, that allows current flow only in 1 direction and blocks in other direction, because of a depletion layer, which creates a potential barrier.
zener diode- special type of pn junction diode designed to operate safely in reverse breakdown, allowing current to flow backward when applied reverse voltage exceeds a specific stable value called zener voltage. this ability to maintain a constant voltage makes zener diode useful as voltage regulator
